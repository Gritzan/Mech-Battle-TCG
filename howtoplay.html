How to Play

Mech Battle Rulebook — Ver 2

Context

You're the General of a military faction and you command a small elite company composed of a Mothership and its Commander and a specialized Mech.

You'll battle other similar companies over control of star systems. During combat, you'll strategically position your Mothership and Mech on the field, upgrade your Mech on the spot and battle for supremacy in a fast paced duel against your enemies.

Objective

The objective of the game is to reach 5 Victory Points before your enemy does so.

  • You gain 1 VP each time you destroy your enemy's Mech.
  • You gain the amount of your enemy's VP when you destroy their Mothership, with a minimum of 1.
  • When you destroy your enemy's Mech and they don't have a Mothership, you gain 2 VP instead.
  • When you try to draw from an empty Deck, your enemy gains 1 VP.

Mounting your Playset

  • 1 Mech, 1 Commander and 1 Mothership card, with up to a maximum of 2 colors combined.
  • A 10-card Energy Deck, with as many copies of Basic Energy cards as you want, and a maximum of 3 copies of each non-Basic Energy card.
  • A 5-card Spec Parts Deck, with a maximum of 1 copy of each card, of the same colors as your Mech, Commander and Mothership.
  • A 40-card Deck, with a maximum of 3 copies of each card, of the same colors as your Mech, Commander and Mothership.

The Field

The Battle Area has 5 spaces. Each player's Mech and Mothership start in the space closest to them; the Commander stays in the base zone with the Deck, Parts and Energy.

Card Types

Motherships

  • Fields: Color, Name, HP, Type, Effect.
  • Start the game in the battlefield, in the allied start space, side by side with your Mech.
  • Have a passive, or an effect that requires tapping to activate.
  • HP value is at the top-left corner.
  • Move in the battlefield by tapping and moving 1 space forward or backward.
  • When destroyed: remove them from the game, together with your Commander.

Commanders

  • Fields: Color, Name, Type, Effect.
  • Start the game in the field, side by side with the Deck.
  • Have a passive, or an effect that requires tapping to activate.
  • When your Mothership is destroyed, remove your Commander from the game with it.

Mechs

  • Fields: Color, Name, Stability, HP, Hardpoints, Type, Effect.
  • Start the game in the battlefield, in the allied start space, side by side with your Mothership.
  • Have a passive, or an effect that requires tapping to activate.
  • HP at the top-left corner; Stability (green) at its side.
  • Hardpoints define how many non-Spec Parts it can have attached at once.
  • Move in the battlefield by tapping and moving 1 space forward or backward.
  • Destroyed (HP reaches 0): destroy one attached non-Spec Part (or a Spec Part if you have no non-Spec Parts attached), then redeploy the Mech at the start of your next Turn, at your Mothership's space (or the Allied start space if you have no Mothership).
  • Staggered (Stability reaches 0): tap it and all its Parts, then Stun one of them. The next time it would take HP damage this turn, it takes double damage.

Spec Parts

  • Fields: Color, Name, Part Type and Energy Cost, Type, Ability.
  • You start the match with Spec Parts whose total Energy Cost is equal to or lower than your Mech's HP, and of different Part Types, attached to your Mech.
  • Have a passive, or an effect that requires tapping to activate.
  • If you redeploy your Mech and 1 or more Spec Parts were destroyed, you may pay their Energy Cost to reattach them during redeploy.

Parts

  • Fields: Color, Name, Total Energy Cost (white), Colored EC below it, Type, Effect.
  • Example: Beam Saber has Total Energy Cost 1, of which 1 must be Red — pay 1 Red Energy to attach it.
  • Have a passive, or an effect that requires tapping to activate. Attach untapped, and can tap the turn they enter.
  • If attaching a Part would exceed your Mech's Hardpoints, scrap one of your Parts. Spec Parts don't count toward the Hardpoints limit.

Actions

  • Fields: Color, Name, Total Energy Cost (white), Colored EC below it, Type, Effect.
  • Example: Perfect Sabotage costs 5 total, of which 2 must be Purple.
  • Have an effect that activates on use.

Drones

  • Fields: Name, HP, Type, Effect.
  • Deployed during the game in the same space as your Mech or Mothership.
  • Have a passive, or an effect that requires tapping to activate.
  • Move in the battlefield by tapping and moving 1 space forward or backward. Deploy them in Play with the + Token button.

Starting a Match

  1. Players present their Motherships, Commanders and Mechs to each other, and position them on their starting spaces in the Field.
  2. Players select their Spec Parts from their Spec Parts Deck, reveal them simultaneously, and attach them to their Mechs in the Field.
  3. Roll a die to decide who plays first. The winner chooses. The second player puts a Generic Energy Token in their Energy Area.
  4. Players shuffle their Deck and Energy Deck, then draw a 5-card starting hand. Starting from the first player, each player keeps their hand or returns up to 2 cards to the bottom of the Deck and draws that many back (Mulligan). If a player returned any cards, shuffle the Deck after.

This is exactly the sequence Play walks you through: roll → first/second → Spec Parts → draw 5 → mulligan.

In the first turn of each player, they Generate Energy twice.

Keywords

Reaction — you may use this card or ability as a reaction to another play; it resolves first.

Block — tap to receive an attack made against another Mothership, Mech or Drone. Must be in range and in the same space as, or in front of, the target.

Destroyed — activates when the Mech, Part, Drone or Mothership is destroyed.

Stun — target doesn't untap in its next Untap Phase.

Scan X — look at the top X cards of your Deck, return each to the top or bottom in any order.

Dodge — tap to dodge a Ranged attack against you (no damage on a successful Dodge).

Deflect X — enemies must pay (X) extra generic Energy to target this card with Actions and Abilities (not Attacks).

Energized — a state allowing special Abilities; an object is Energized when it has a Charge counter.

Lock On — if the target doesn't have a Lock counter, add one.

Push X / Pull X — move target Mech or Drone X spaces away from / toward your Mech or Mothership.

Melee / Short / Long — can only target objects in the same space / 1 space away / 2 spaces away.

Piercing — also hits another target in the same space or 1 space behind the original target.

Area — hits all enemy objects in the same space as the target.

Thermal / Kinetic / Electric — damage types that reduce a Mech's HP.

Stability — damage that reduces a Mech's Stability value.
